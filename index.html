Class formats

Every lesson runs 50 to 60 minutes, taught live on ChessLang, the academy's AI-based lesson platform. Group and semi-private classes follow a fixed timetable; one-to-one lessons are arranged flexibly. If a student misses a class, they join a live makeup session in another batch.

Group

Learn together

Maximum 8–10 students per batch. Eight sessions a month on a fixed timetable. Students play real games after almost every class, and the academy runs one tournament every week.

Semi-private

More attention

Maximum 3–4 students per batch. Eight sessions a month on a fixed timetable. A good fit for children who need more individual attention than a group allows.

One-to-one

Your pace, your goals

One child, one coach, arranged flexibly around your family's schedule. Eight sessions a month, with lessons shaped to the student's level and goals.

A clear curriculum, in five levels

Good chess teaching follows a clear path. Each level builds on the last, and every idea is used in real games straight away.

  1. Checkmates — learning how games are won.
  2. Winning Material — learning how to gain an advantage.
  3. Mastering Defensive Technique — learning how to hold a position and stay safe.
  4. Sacrifice to Win — learning when giving something up wins the game.
  5. Finding Winning Moves — learning how to spot the best move in a position.

Child safety comes first

Before you enrol your child anywhere, confirm every item on this checklist. These are the standards followed by Superior Chess Academy.

  • Teacher identity + criminal background verified
  • Parent/guardian identity verified + consent
  • Secure online classrooms, restricted access
  • Parents can access classes + progress
  • No unauthorised private teacher–child communication
  • Safeguarding policy + teacher code of conduct
  • Complaint reporting + incident management system
  • Student data privacy + protection compliance
